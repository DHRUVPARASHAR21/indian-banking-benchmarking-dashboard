# Methodology

## Reporting period and units

The base period is FY2025, ending March 2025. Monetary values are presented in ₹ crore. The workbook calculates KPIs from the standardised input dataset rather than treating ranking values as hard-coded outputs.

## Calculated KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NIM | NII ÷ average interest-earning assets | Higher generally indicates stronger net spread, subject to funding and credit-risk context. |
| ROA | Net profit ÷ average assets | A broad measure of profitability per rupee of assets. |
| ROE | Net profit ÷ average equity | Return generated for equity holders. |
| Cost / income | Operating expense ÷ operating income | Lower usually indicates better operating efficiency. |
| CASA ratio | CASA deposits ÷ total deposits | Higher CASA can reduce funding cost. |
| Credit cost | Provisions ÷ average gross advances | Lower usually indicates lower realised credit loss, but must be assessed with coverage and risk mix. |
| GNPA | Gross NPAs ÷ gross advances | Lower indicates stronger asset quality. |
| NNPA | Net NPAs ÷ net advances | Lower indicates lower residual stressed-asset exposure after provisions. |
| Loan growth | (FY25 advances − FY24 advances) ÷ FY24 advances | Growth in lending. |
| Deposit growth | (FY25 deposits − FY24 deposits) ÷ FY24 deposits | Growth in funding base. |

## Interest-earning-assets proxy

Not every bank discloses an identical average interest-earning-assets measure. For consistent peer comparison, the workbook approximates it as the average of gross advances plus investments across FY2024 and FY2025. This produces a directional NIM benchmark rather than a replacement for each bank's reported NIM.

## Peer-statistics convention

- **Peer median:** the median of all 10 bank observations.
- **Top quartile:** the 75th percentile of all 10 observations.
- **Ranking direction:** ROA, ROE, NIM and growth are ranked high to low. Cost / income and GNPA are ranked low to high.

## Comparability controls

Before extending or refreshing the model, verify the following:

1. Standalone versus consolidated reporting basis.
2. Unit consistency, including ₹ crore versus ₹ lakh crore.
3. Whether advances and deposit figures include the same entity perimeter.
4. Whether provision expense includes one-off or investment-related items.
5. Whether employee count is disclosed consistently enough for productivity comparison.
6. Whether bank-reported GNPA, NNPA, PCR and capital ratios use the same period-end date.

## Interpretation discipline

No KPI should be read alone. High growth needs funding and capital support. High NIM should be evaluated against GNPA, NNPA, PCR and credit cost. Low cost / income is most useful when it is sustainable and does not coincide with weakening franchise indicators.
