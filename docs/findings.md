# FY2025 analytical findings

## How to use these findings

These are prompts for further analysis, not investment recommendations. Validate material conclusions against the source filing and current-quarter update.

## 1. Profitability should be read with efficiency

The dashboard ranks banks by ROA, ROE and NIM. A strong ROA can result from operating efficiency, asset mix, funding cost, lower credit cost or a combination of these. Compare ROA with cost / income and credit cost before assigning a quality conclusion.

## 2. Funding is the key growth constraint

Loan growth that materially exceeds deposit growth can raise reliance on wholesale or market funding. Use the loan-growth ranking alongside deposit growth and CASA ratio to identify whether a bank's growth is matched by durable deposits.

## 3. Asset quality remains a differentiator

GNPA and NNPA identify the level of stressed loans, while PCR and credit cost help assess loss absorption and new stress formation. A bank can have a low GNPA ratio but a rising credit cost; that combination deserves separate review.

## 4. Capital determines growth capacity

CET1, Tier 1 and CAR frame the ability to sustain growth and absorb losses. Compare capital ratios with loan growth and ROE. A high capital buffer may be strategically valuable, but weak returns on that capital can be a separate issue.

## Example dashboard reading: HDFC Bank

The FY2025 model illustrates how to compare HDFC Bank's cost / income ratio with the peer median, then check CASA, NIM, GNPA and loan-growth positions to decide whether the apparent efficiency result is supported by funding and risk metrics. The point is the workflow, not a standalone recommendation.
