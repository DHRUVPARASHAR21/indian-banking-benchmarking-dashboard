# Banking glossary

**CASA:** Current-account and savings-account deposits. These deposits are often lower-cost than term deposits.

**CAR / CRAR:** Capital adequacy ratio, measuring regulatory capital against risk-weighted assets.

**CET1:** Common Equity Tier 1, the highest-quality regulatory capital.

**Credit cost:** Provision expense divided by average loans. It is a practical measure of loss absorbed during the period.

**GNPA:** Gross non-performing assets as a percentage of gross advances.

**NIM:** Net interest margin. It measures net interest income against interest-earning assets.

**NNPA:** Net non-performing assets after provisions, as a percentage of net advances.

**PCR:** Provision coverage ratio. Higher coverage can provide greater protection against future loss recognition.

**ROA:** Return on assets. It measures profit generated from the asset base.

**ROE:** Return on equity. It measures profit generated from equity capital.

**Tier 1 capital:** Core capital that can absorb losses while the bank continues operating.
